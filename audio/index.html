Bench Audio Toolkit

Private audio tools that run in your browser

Convert, compress, trim, join, clean, record, analyse and inspect audio without uploading it. Every tool below has a real client-side handler and a tested download path. Tools that are not ready are labelled honestly instead of pretending to work.

Quick answer

The Bench Audio Toolkit is a free set of 48 browser-based audio tools: 43 ready tools and 5 experimental tools. Decoding, editing and encoding run locally with the Web Audio API and a same-origin FFmpeg.wasm engine, so audio is not uploaded.

Convert & Compress

Change format, bitrate and size between MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, OGG, Opus, M4A and AIFF.

Audio Converter Convert between MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC/M4A, OGG, Opus and AIFF with a real encoder and an exact output size. Audio Compressor Reduce an audio file's size by lowering bitrate, downmixing to mono and trimming the sample rate. Audio to WAV Decode any supported audio file and export a universally compatible, uncompressed WAV file. Audio to MP3 Encode any audio file to MP3 with libmp3lame at a bitrate you choose. Audio to FLAC Encode audio to lossless, compressed FLAC - smaller than WAV with no quality loss. Audio to M4A (AAC) Encode audio to AAC in an M4A container - small files with good quality on Apple devices. Audio to OGG / Opus Encode audio to open OGG Vorbis or Opus, ideal for the web and games. Audio to AIFF Convert audio to uncompressed AIFF, the classic Apple lossless audio format. Ringtone Maker Crop a song to a 30-second ringtone, add a fade and export as MP3 or M4A.

Cut, Join & Edit

Trim, split, merge, fade, reverse, change speed and loudness.

Audio Trimmer Cut a section out of an audio file with an interactive waveform and export the exact range. Audio Joiner Merge two or more audio files into one track, in the order you choose, with optional crossfade. Audio Splitter Split one file into several parts by time or into equal-length chunks, exported together. Fade In / Out Add a fade-in, a fade-out or both to smooth the start and end of a track. Reverse Audio Play an audio file backwards - useful for effects, hidden messages and creative edits. Change Speed Speed up or slow down audio while keeping the pitch the same (time-stretch). Pitch Shifter Shift the pitch up or down. Speakers' pitch can be raised or lowered for anonymity or effects. Experimental Loop Maker Repeat a section of audio a chosen number of times to build a smooth loop. Crossfade Editor Blend two tracks together with a real crossfade at a duration you choose. Stereo & Channel Tools Swap or merge channels, isolate left or right, downmix to mono or make a dual-mono track.

Clean & Enhance

Reduce noise and hum, normalise loudness, EQ, limit and detect clipping.

Silence Remover Detect silent gaps and remove them, or keep the gaps but trim the leading and trailing silence. Volume & Normalise Change the loudness by a fixed amount or normalise to a target peak, with clipping protection. Noise Reducer Reduce steady background noise (hiss, hum, air conditioning) with a real FFT denoiser. Experimental Voice Enhancer Brighten and control speech with a high-pass, EQ and gentle compression chain. Experimental Bass, Treble & EQ Boost or cut bass, treble and a selectable mid band with real peaking filters. Loudness Normaliser (LUFS) Normalise to a broadcast loudness target in LUFS with true-peak limiting.

Record & Microphone

Record from your microphone locally, meter input and test channels.

Voice Recorder Record from your microphone in the browser, preview it and download a WAV or WebM/Opus file. Microphone Check Test whether your microphone works, see a live input-level meter and confirm permission status. Stereo Channel Test Play a signal through each channel to confirm left/right speakers and headphones are wired correctly.

Music & Sound

Generate tones and noise, analyse tempo and spectrum, visualise waveforms.

Tone Generator Generate a pure sine tone or a musical note at a chosen frequency and length, then download it. Noise Generator Generate white, pink or brown noise for masking, sleep or testing. Metronome A click track with adjustable tempo, time signature and accent for practice. BPM & Key Detector Estimate the tempo (BPM) of a track and suggest a likely musical key from its spectrum. Experimental Spectrogram / Spectrum Analyser Render a spectrogram or real-time spectrum of a file to see its frequencies over time.

Metadata & Diagnostics

Inspect and edit tags, measure loudness, peaks, bitrate and integrity.

Clipping Detector Scan a file for clipped samples and report where and how often it distorts. Silence Detector Map every silent gap in a file with start time, end time and duration. Audio Metadata Viewer Inspect ID3/tags, duration, bitrate, sample rate, channels and codec of an audio file. ID3 Tag Editor View and rewrite common ID3/tag fields (title, artist, album, genre) and export a new copy. Experimental Loudness & Peak Analyser Measure integrated LUFS, true peak, RMS and dynamic range for broadcast or streaming checks. Audio Integrity Check Verify a file decodes cleanly and report header, signature and structure problems. Duplicate Audio Finder Compare several audio files and find exact duplicates using a content hash.

Developer Utilities

Base64, data URIs, waveform images, player snippets and batch export.

Audio to Base64 Encode an audio file as a Base64 string or a data URI for embedding in code. Base64 to Audio Decode a Base64 string or data URI back into a playable, downloadable audio file. Audio Batch Rename Rename and export several audio files at once with a prefix, sequence numbers and a chosen format. Audio to ZIP Bundle several audio files into a single ZIP archive for one-click download. Waveform Image Generate a clean waveform image (PNG) from any audio file for video, print or the web. Audio MIME & Signature Checker See the real file type from its magic bytes and compare it with the extension and reported MIME type. HTML Audio Player Generator Generate a safe, accessible HTML <audio> player snippet for your file. Podcast RSS Enclosure Validator Check a podcast RSS feed's <enclosure> tags for missing or invalid audio URLs and types.

Status and limits

Implemented tools are covered by automated tests and produce real files. Experimental tools run real signal processing but have documented quality caveats; their results should be checked before important use. Limits: 100 MB per file, 30 minutes per file, 20 files per batch. Conversion and complex filters use a 31 MB FFmpeg engine downloaded once and cached.

How processing works

Simple editing, analysis, recording and WAV export use the Web Audio API directly in the tab. Format conversion and complex filters (loudness normalisation, denoise, speed, EQ) use a WebAssembly build of FFmpeg that runs on your device. Either way, the audio bytes never leave your machine and Bench keeps no copy.

Licences and source

The FFmpeg engine shipped here is the official @ffmpeg/core build, licensed under the GNU General Public License v2.0 or later. It is served unmodified from this site at /vendor/ffmpeg/ and is never loaded from a third-party CDN. The JavaScript wrapper (@ffmpeg/ffmpeg) is MIT licensed.

Full attributions, exact version numbers and a written offer to supply the corresponding source code are published in /vendor/ffmpeg/NOTICE.txt. That notice names the upstream FFmpeg project and the ffmpeg.wasm packaging project, including their source repositories.

Quick answers about the audio toolkit

Are my audio files uploaded when I use the Bench Audio Toolkit?

No. Decoding, editing and encoding run inside your browser. Conversion and heavy filters use a same-origin FFmpeg.wasm engine that runs on your device; audio bytes are never sent to Bench or a third party.

How many audio tools are available?

48 public audio tools are available: 43 implemented tools and 5 experimental tools. Tools that need a model or an unreliable browser API are listed as deferred or unavailable instead of being faked.

Do the audio tools cost anything or need an account?

No. The tools are free and no account, password or subscription is required.

What are the audio tool limits?

Each file can be up to 100 MB and 30 minutes, with 20 files per batch. The FFmpeg engine is about 31 MB and is cached after the first download.
