What it does

Select a section of a track, apply a short fade in/out and export a phone-ready ringtone.

Tool-specific detail

Ringtone Maker is scoped to Turn your favourite section of a song into a ringtone: it accepts MP3, WAV and other supported formats, produces audio, and exposes only the controls that job needs. This tool edits audio only; installing a ringtone on iOS still requires iTunes/Finder or a ringtone app.

How to use

  1. Add a song.
  2. Set the start time and length (30 s is the iOS limit).
  3. Choose MP3 or M4A and add a fade.
  4. Export and transfer the ringtone to your phone.

Common use cases

  • Turn your favourite section of a song into a ringtone.
  • Make a custom alert tone.

Limits & considerations

This tool edits audio only; installing a ringtone on iOS still requires iTunes/Finder or a ringtone app. Respect copyright when using commercial music.

How your audio is processed

Ringtone Maker uses FFmpeg.wasm on this device to create audio. The engine is loaded from this site on first use and cached afterwards; no source audio is uploaded.

Privacy & security

Ringtone Maker reads MP3, WAV and other supported formats from local memory, creates audio with browser APIs, and keeps the result in a temporary object URL until you download it or leave the page. No audio bytes are uploaded, logged, or stored by Bench.
