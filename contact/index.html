Feature Suggestions & Bug Reports

Progeni Bench is constantly expanding its suite of client-side web tools. Let us know if you have an idea for a utility that can save time without uploading files to a cloud server.

When reporting a bug, include the tool name, your browser and version, and the steps that triggered the problem. Since every tool runs entirely in your browser, these details let us reproduce the issue without ever asking for your files.

Why there is no contact form

We deliberately run this site without a web contact form. A form would require a server-side endpoint to receive your message — an extra place where data is collected and retained. Email goes straight from your mail provider to our inbox, which keeps the data path as short as the tools themselves keep yours.

We usually respond to all inquiries within 24 to 48 business hours.
