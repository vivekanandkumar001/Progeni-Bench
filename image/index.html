Quick answer

The Bench Image Toolkit is a free set of 42 browser-based image tools: 39 ready tools, 3 experimental tools and a batch Image Studio. Images are decoded, edited and encoded locally with Canvas, so the files are not uploaded.

Compress & Optimize

Smaller files without guesswork.

Image Compressor Shrink JPEG, PNG, WebP and AVIF images with real before/after sizes. Drop several files to compress them in one pass. Compress to Target Size Hit a 10 KB, 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB, 200 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB or custom size by searching quality and, if allowed, dimensions.

Resize & Prepare

Exact dimensions, ratios and application presets.

Resize Image Resize by pixels or percentage with aspect-ratio lock, fit, fill or stretch, and optional batch output. Crop Image Crop with a percentage box, fixed aspect ratios, rounded corners or a true circular crop with transparent corners. Application Photo & Signature Presets Prepare passport, visa, UPSC, SSC, NEET, JEE, GATE and signature images with configurable size presets and a target KB limit. Social Media & YouTube Image Sizes Crop and export images for YouTube thumbnails and banners, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X presets. DPI Inspector & Setter Read the embedded DPI/PPI of PNG and JPEG files, inspect pixel dimensions, and write a corrected DPI copy. Photo & Signature Composition Combine an application photo and a signature into one canvas with a configurable layout and export size. Experimental

Convert

Change format, PDF, data URI and vector rasterisation.

Image Converter Convert between JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, BMP, TIFF, GIF and ICO, and rasterise SVG, with batch output and transparent handling. Image to PDF Combine JPEG, PNG, WebP or rasterised SVG images into a single PDF, one page per image or fitted to A4/Letter. Base64 & Data URI Converter Encode an image to Base64 or a data URI, or decode Base64 back into a real, decodable image file.

Edit & Design

Crop, colour, annotations, collage and composition.

Rotate, Flip & Straighten Rotate by 90-degree steps, flip horizontally or vertically, or straighten a tilted photo with automatic canvas expansion. Colour Adjustments Adjust brightness, contrast, saturation and hue, or apply grayscale, sepia and inversion, with an original/result comparison. Blur, Pixelate & Redact Blur, pixelate or paint over a rectangular region to hide sensitive details, with no upload to a server. Text, Captions & Annotations Add a caption, label, callout text or a caption bar to an image with real canvas rendering. Border, Padding & Rounded Corners Add a border, outer padding, rounded corners, a colour or gradient canvas and an optional drop shadow. Visible Watermark Add visible text watermarks with configurable placement, opacity, rotation, tiling and colour. Meme Generator Turn any image into a classic top/bottom caption meme with outlined text, local processing and instant download. Collage Maker Arrange multiple images into a clean grid collage with configurable columns, gap, background and output width. Image Splitter & Grid Cutter Split an image into a rows x columns grid of equal tiles and download every tile in a ZIP. Side-by-Side Image Merge Merge two or more images horizontally or vertically with an optional gap and background colour. Image Compare & Similarity Compare two images visually and numerically with dimensions, exact hashes and a perceptual similarity score. Product Photo Shadow & Background Place a product cut-out on a clean background with padding, rounded corners and a soft realistic drop shadow.

Documents & Scan

Clean scans and document workflows.

Scan Cleanup & Deskew Convert phone photos of documents into cleaner grayscale or binarised pages and correct a manually measured tilt. Experimental

Metadata & Privacy

Inspect, strip, hash and compare.

Metadata & EXIF Viewer Inspect EXIF, GPS, camera details, dimensions, transparency, colour profile presence and embedded DPI in JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP and TIFF files. EXIF & GPS Remover Remove EXIF, GPS, camera and embedded metadata by re-encoding the image, then verify the exported file no longer carries a JPEG EXIF block. Image Hash Generator Generate exact SHA-256 hashes plus perceptual average and difference hashes for image identity and similarity checks. Duplicate & Similar Photo Finder Find byte-identical duplicates and visually similar photos using SHA-256 and perceptual hashing, entirely in the browser. Colour Palette & Histogram Extract a dominant colour palette, inspect RGB/luminance histograms and sample colours from an image.

Create & Developer

Icons, social cards, QR, SVG and srcset.

Favicon & App Icon Generator Generate 16, 32, 48, 96, 180, 192 and 512 px PNG icons, a multi-entry ICO and a web manifest snippet from one square image. Open Graph Image Generator Compose a 1200 x 630 social preview card from an image with a headline, subtitle, overlay and accent bar. QR Code Generator Generate a real QR code for text or a URL with size, quiet zone, colours and error-correction control, as PNG or SVG. QR Code Reader Decode a QR code from a screenshot or photo locally in the browser, with no image upload. Barcode Generator Generate CODE128, EAN-13, EAN-8, CODE39 or UPC-A barcodes as real PNG images with validation. SVG Optimiser & Validator Remove scripts, event handlers and dangerous URLs, validate basic SVG structure, and minify markup for safer embedding. Image Placeholder Generator Generate clean SVG or PNG placeholders with exact dimensions, custom colours and a label. Responsive Image srcset Generator Generate multiple resized WebP, JPEG or PNG widths plus a ready-to-use srcset and sizes snippet.

Animation & Batch

GIF, sprite sheets and bulk exports.

GIF Studio: Create & Extract Build an animated GIF from a sequence of images, or extract every frame from an existing GIF, with frame delay and resize controls. Sprite Sheet Generator Pack images into an evenly spaced sprite sheet with a generated CSS background-position snippet. Batch Rename & Export Rename a batch of images with a numbered pattern and optionally convert format, then download everything as a ZIP.

Diagnostics

Understand why an image fails.

Image Inspector & Diagnostics Check real file signatures, decodability, dimensions, transparency, orientation and compression advice, and get a plain-English failure diagnosis. Corruption Recovery (Best Effort) Attempt a best-effort decode and re-encode of a damaged image, with an honest report when recovery is impossible. Experimental

Status and limits

Implemented tools are covered by automated tests and produce real files. Experimental tools run real pixel work but have limited validation; their results should be checked before important use. Limits: 50 MB per file, 40 megapixels per image, 30 files per batch, and a maximum dimension of 12000 px.

Quick answers about the image toolkit

Are my images uploaded when I use the Bench Image Toolkit?

No. Decoding, editing and encoding run inside your browser using Canvas and File APIs. Image bytes are not sent to Bench or a third party.

How many image tools are available?

42 public image tools are available: 39 implemented tools and 3 experimental tools. Image Studio adds a batch resize, convert and ZIP workflow.

Do the image tools cost anything or need an account?

No. The tools are free and no account, password or subscription is required.

What are the image tool limits?

Each file can be up to 50 MB and 40 megapixels, with 30 files per batch and a maximum dimension of 12000 px.
