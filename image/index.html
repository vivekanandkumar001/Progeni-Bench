Bench Image Toolkit

Private image tools that run in your browser

Compress, resize, convert, edit, inspect and generate images without uploading them. Every tool below has a real client-side handler and a tested download path. Tools that are not ready are labelled honestly instead of pretending to work.

Compress & Optimize

Smaller files without guesswork.

Image Compressor Shrink JPEG, PNG, WebP and AVIF images with real before/after sizes. Drop several files to compress them in one pass. Implemented and tested Compress to Target Size Hit a 10 KB, 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB, 200 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB or custom size by searching quality and, if allowed, dimensions. Implemented and tested

Resize & Prepare

Exact dimensions, ratios and application presets.

Resize Image Resize by pixels or percentage with aspect-ratio lock, fit, fill or stretch, and optional batch output. Implemented and tested Crop Image Crop with a percentage box, fixed aspect ratios, rounded corners or a true circular crop with transparent corners. Implemented and tested Application Photo & Signature Presets Prepare passport, visa, UPSC, SSC, NEET, JEE, GATE and signature images with configurable size presets and a target KB limit. Implemented and tested Social Media & YouTube Image Sizes Crop and export images for YouTube thumbnails and banners, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X presets. Implemented and tested DPI Inspector & Setter Read the embedded DPI/PPI of PNG and JPEG files, inspect pixel dimensions, and write a corrected DPI copy. Implemented and tested Photo & Signature Composition Combine an application photo and a signature into one canvas with a configurable layout and export size. Experimental

Convert

Change format, PDF, data URI and vector rasterisation.

Image Converter Convert between JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, BMP, TIFF, GIF and ICO, and rasterise SVG, with batch output and transparent handling. Implemented and tested Image to PDF Combine JPEG, PNG, WebP or rasterised SVG images into a single PDF, one page per image or fitted to A4/Letter. Implemented and tested Base64 & Data URI Converter Encode an image to Base64 or a data URI, or decode Base64 back into a real, decodable image file. Implemented and tested

Edit & Design

Crop, colour, annotations, collage and composition.

Rotate, Flip & Straighten Rotate by 90-degree steps, flip horizontally or vertically, or straighten a tilted photo with automatic canvas expansion. Implemented and tested Colour Adjustments Adjust brightness, contrast, saturation and hue, or apply grayscale, sepia and inversion, with an original/result comparison. Implemented and tested Blur, Pixelate & Redact Blur, pixelate or paint over a rectangular region to hide sensitive details, with no upload to a server. Implemented and tested Text, Captions & Annotations Add a caption, label, callout text or a caption bar to an image with real canvas rendering. Implemented and tested Border, Padding & Rounded Corners Add a border, outer padding, rounded corners, a colour or gradient canvas and an optional drop shadow. Implemented and tested Visible Watermark Add visible text watermarks with configurable placement, opacity, rotation, tiling and colour. Implemented and tested Meme Generator Turn any image into a classic top/bottom caption meme with outlined text, local processing and instant download. Implemented and tested Collage Maker Arrange multiple images into a clean grid collage with configurable columns, gap, background and output width. Implemented and tested Image Splitter & Grid Cutter Split an image into a rows x columns grid of equal tiles and download every tile in a ZIP. Implemented and tested Side-by-Side Image Merge Merge two or more images horizontally or vertically with an optional gap and background colour. Implemented and tested Image Compare & Similarity Compare two images visually and numerically with dimensions, exact hashes and a perceptual similarity score. Implemented and tested Product Photo Shadow & Background Place a product cut-out on a clean background with padding, rounded corners and a soft realistic drop shadow. Implemented and tested

Documents & Scan

Clean scans and document workflows.

Scan Cleanup & Deskew Convert phone photos of documents into cleaner grayscale or binarised pages and correct a manually measured tilt. Experimental

Metadata & Privacy

Inspect, strip, hash and compare.

Metadata & EXIF Viewer Inspect EXIF, GPS, camera details, dimensions, transparency, colour profile presence and embedded DPI in JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP and TIFF files. Implemented and tested EXIF & GPS Remover Remove EXIF, GPS, camera and embedded metadata by re-encoding the image, then verify the exported file no longer carries a JPEG EXIF block. Implemented and tested Image Hash Generator Generate exact SHA-256 hashes plus perceptual average and difference hashes for image identity and similarity checks. Implemented and tested Duplicate & Similar Photo Finder Find byte-identical duplicates and visually similar photos using SHA-256 and perceptual hashing, entirely in the browser. Implemented and tested Colour Palette & Histogram Extract a dominant colour palette, inspect RGB/luminance histograms and sample colours from an image. Implemented and tested

Icons, social cards, QR, SVG and srcset.

Favicon & App Icon Generator Generate 16, 32, 48, 96, 180, 192 and 512 px PNG icons, a multi-entry ICO and a web manifest snippet from one square image. Implemented and tested Open Graph Image Generator Compose a 1200 x 630 social preview card from an image with a headline, subtitle, overlay and accent bar. Implemented and tested QR Code Generator Generate a real QR code for text or a URL with size, quiet zone, colours and error-correction control, as PNG or SVG. Implemented and tested QR Code Reader Decode a QR code from a screenshot or photo locally in the browser, with no image upload. Implemented and tested Barcode Generator Generate CODE128, EAN-13, EAN-8, CODE39 or UPC-A barcodes as real PNG images with validation. Implemented and tested SVG Optimizer & Validator Remove scripts, event handlers and dangerous URLs, validate basic SVG structure, and minify markup for safer embedding. Implemented and tested Image Placeholder Generator Generate clean SVG or PNG placeholders with exact dimensions, custom colours and a label. Implemented and tested Responsive Image srcset Generator Generate multiple resized WebP, JPEG or PNG widths plus a ready-to-use srcset and sizes snippet. Implemented and tested

GIF, sprite sheets and bulk exports.

GIF Studio: Create & Extract Build an animated GIF from a sequence of images, or extract every frame from an existing GIF, with frame delay and resize controls. Implemented and tested Sprite Sheet Generator Pack images into an evenly spaced sprite sheet with a generated CSS background-position snippet. Diagnostics

Understand why an image fails.

Image Inspector & Diagnostics Check real file signatures, decodability, dimensions, transparency, orientation and compression advice, and get a plain-English failure diagnosis. Implemented and tested Corruption Recovery (Best Effort) Attempt a best-effort decode and re-encode of a damaged image, with an honest report when recovery is impossible. Experimental

Status and limits

Implemented tools are covered by automated tests and produce real files. Experimental tools run real pixel work but have limited validation; their results should be checked before important use. Limits: 50 MB per file, 40 megapixels per image, 30 files per batch, and a maximum dimension of 12000 px.

    These entries exist so the catalogue stays honest. No page is generated for them and no upload control is offered.

    Image to Text OCRDeferred

    An OCR engine and language pack are not bundled. Shipping Tesseract.js with an English model adds roughly 10-15 MB to the static deployment and needs a dedicated loading, cancellation and accuracy test pass. Deferred rather than faked.

    Searchable PDFDeferred

    Depends on the OCR engine and a PDF text-layer writer. Neither is available in the current dependency set.

    PDF to ImageDeferred

    PDF decoding requires PDF.js or a server engine. Adding a full PDF renderer for one tool was not justified in this build.

    HEIC / HEIF ConverterDeferred

    No HEIC decoder is bundled. Browser support is inconsistent and libheif adds a large WASM payload; files are rejected with an explanation instead of producing a broken output.

    Video to GIFDeferred

    Requires video seeking, frame extraction and a bounded duration workflow. Image-only GIF tools are implemented; video decoding is deferred.

    Barcode ReaderDeferred

    The bundled QR decoder does not read 1D barcodes. A separate decoder was not added without a tested implementation.

    HTML to ImageDeferred

    Requires an HTML/CSS rasteriser with strict sanitisation. Deferred to avoid an unsafe rendering surface.

    Raster to SVG Vector TraceDeferred

    Not implemented. Bench does not embed a raster image inside SVG and call it vector tracing.

    CMYK / ICC ConversionDeferred

    Canvas is sRGB and does not expose colour-managed CMYK transforms. A correct implementation needs a colour-management engine and profile handling.

    Image URL InspectorDeferred

    Remote fetching would create SSRF and CORS risks, and the project has no isolated fetch service. The tool is intentionally unavailable.
