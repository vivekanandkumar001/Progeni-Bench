Quick answers about Progeni Bench

What is Progeni Bench?

Progeni Bench is a free collection of 122 browser-based file utilities: 32 core tools, 42 image tools, and 48 audio tools. Tool pages identify experimental features and explain their limits.

Are my files uploaded when I use a tool?

Most tools process files locally without uploading them. PDF Translator keeps PDF pages in your browser but sends extracted text to DeepL for translation.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No account or subscription is required to use the browser tools. Open a tool, select a local file when needed, and download its result.

What can I do with Progeni Bench?

You can prepare print and craft projects, work with image and audio files, clean CSV or contact data, and inspect or repair subtitles. Browse the core, image, and audio toolkits to find a tool for a specific task.
