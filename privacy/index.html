Privacy Policy

1. Local Processing and the Translation Exception

Most Progeni Bench tools are a client-side execution platform. When you use bookmark cleaners, CSV tools, image converters, subtitle editors, or calendar organizers:

  • Your uploaded files are loaded into your browser's local memory (RAM) via standard HTML5 File APIs.
  • Calculations and conversions run on your device's CPU/GPU.
  • Generated outputs are downloaded directly to your disk.
  • Those tools do not send selected file contents to Bench servers.

PDF Translator is different. The PDF itself remains in your browser, but extracted text blocks are sent through a Vercel Function to DeepL for translation. DeepL processes that text under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not submit confidential or sensitive documents. The function does not log extracted content; it may retain a successful batch response in volatile server memory for up to five minutes to avoid duplicate translation charges. No PDF or text is stored in a persistent Bench database.

When local OCR is enabled for a scanned PDF, the browser may download English or Hindi OCR language data from the Tesseract data host. Page images are processed locally and are not sent to that host.

2. Zero Third-Party Tracking

Progeni Bench does not embed third-party advertising tracking scripts or behavioral profilers. Most utilities function locally once loaded; PDF Translator needs an internet connection for DeepL translation and OCR language-data downloads.
