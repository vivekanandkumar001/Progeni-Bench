Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. The Zero-Upload Guarantee

Progeni is designed from the ground up as a client-side execution platform. When you use our bookmark cleaners, CSV tools, image converters, subtitle editors, or calendar organizers:

  • Your uploaded files are loaded into your browser's local memory (RAM) via standard HTML5 File APIs.
  • Calculations and conversions run on your device's CPU/GPU.
  • Generated outputs (such as cleaned CSVs, PNG sheets, or ICS files) are downloaded directly to your disk.
  • None of your files, images, or document contents are ever sent to our servers or stored on remote databases.

2. Local Storage and Preferences

Progeni does not use tracking cookies or third-party web analytics. We use your browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly to remember client-side preferences (such as dismissing informative banners). No personal data or browsing behavior is tracked or transmitted.

3. Zero Third-Party Tracking

Progeni does not embed third-party advertising tracking scripts or behavioral profilers. All utility tools function entirely offline once loaded into your web browser.

4. External Links

Our website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external websites.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page or email privacy@progeni.live.
