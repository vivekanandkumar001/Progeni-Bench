About this tool — how it works, limits & FAQ

What it does

Extracts valid HTTP and HTTPS links from complex bookmark exports and compiles them into a clean, minimalist, offline HTML reading list document. Filters out dangerous URI schemes and excessive nested folder tags, creating an uncluttered reading dashboard for long-form articles.

Common use cases

  • Reading List Digest Creation: Convert cluttered browser bookmark folders into a clean Markdown checklist for reading later in Obsidian or Notion.
  • Domain-Grouped Research Lists: Group saved research bookmarks by website domain to organize sources for research papers and articles.
  • Curated Resource Sharing: Format saved web links into shareable text summaries for team wikis, newsletters, and email digests.

How to use

  1. Upload your browser's exported bookmarks.html file into the reading list converter.
  2. The tool sanitizes entries, automatically filtering out javascript: and data: URIs.
  3. Preview the clean list of saved articles and reading links rendered on screen.
  4. Download the standalone reading-list.html document for distraction-free offline reading.

Limits & considerations

Filters out internal browser schemes (javascript:, data:, chrome:, file:). Does not scrape or archive the full offline text content of linked web pages.

Frequently asked questions

What output formats can I generate from my bookmarks?

You can copy or download your reading list as clean Markdown, structured HTML, or plain text link summaries.

Does the tool group links by website domain?

Yes. You can toggle domain-based grouping to categorize articles and tools by their source publication.

Can I copy the formatted reading list directly to my clipboard?

Yes. A single-click copy button allows instant pasting into your favorite note-taking application or document editor.

Privacy & security

Your saved articles and reading links never leave your browser sandbox. Bench reads the bookmark file locally, keeps the parsed list in memory, and exports a standalone HTML reading list.
