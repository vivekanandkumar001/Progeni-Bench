Frequently Asked Questions

How does the tool match colors to real embroidery thread?

Each pixel cluster is mapped via Euclidean color distance to the official DMC 6-strand cotton floss color catalogue with verified hex codes.

What stitch count fabric is used for physical dimension estimates?

Calculations default to standard 14-count Aida (14 stitches per inch), providing exact finished project dimensions in centimeters and inches.

Can I limit the total number of colors in my pattern?

Yes. The quantization slider lets you restrict the palette between 2 and 64 colors to simplify complex needlework projects.
