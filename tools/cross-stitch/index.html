About this tool — how it works, limits & FAQ

What it does

Runs a full Pattern Studio engine. Your image is average-pooled into a stitch grid 20–200 stitches wide (rows follow the aspect ratio), reduced to 2–64 colours with median-cut quantisation, and optionally snapped to a real DMC embroidery floss palette of 300 numbered threads, so every cell carries a genuine floss code and name. Cells can be drawn as flat colour or as a symbol chart (each palette entry gets a stable glyph) over a light cell grid with heavy lines every 10 stitches, and an optional Floyd–Steinberg dithering pass smooths gradients. The legend lists the DMC code, thread name, swatch, chart symbol, stitch count, percentage and an estimated skein count (~1,700 stitches per skein), and the tool reports the finished size on 14-count Aida. Exports a PNG stretched to at least 2,000 px wide with the aspect ratio preserved, a CSV shopping list, and a black-and-white symbol-only print mode.

Common use cases

  • Photo to Cross-Stitch Conversion: Turn family portraits and pet photos into charted needlepoint patterns with authentic DMC embroidery floss codes.
  • Pixel Art & Sprite Stitching: Convert retro gaming sprites and 8-bit graphics into clean 14-count Aida cloth stitching guides.
  • Embroidery Project Floss Budgeting: Generate complete skein estimates and symbol keys before purchasing physical needlework supplies.

How to use

  1. Drop a photo or illustration (PNG, JPEG or WebP) into the upload box — processing stays entirely in your browser.
  2. Set the stitch width (20–200) and maximum colours (2–64); the grid rows follow your image's aspect ratio automatically.
  3. Choose whether to snap colours to the DMC floss palette (recommended for real thread matching) and toggle symbol chart, 10×10 heavy grid lines, dithering or the black-and-white symbol-only print mode.
  4. Check the live preview and legend — every colour shows its DMC code, name, chart symbol, stitch count percentage and estimated skeins, plus the physical size on 14-count Aida.
  5. Download the chart PNG, export the CSV floss shopping list, or press Print for a symbol chart plus legend you can stitch from.

Limits & considerations

Charts are capped at a 200×200 cell grid (40,000 stitches) so the preview stays responsive; very large or very tall photos are automatically reduced to fit. Colour matches are nearest-neighbour in RGB against the built-in DMC table, which is accurate to typical floss shades but not a lab-grade match to a specific dye lot. Dithering is available for cross-stitch charts only, and the symbol set repeats after 64 colours.

Frequently asked questions

How does the tool match colors to real embroidery thread?

Each pixel cluster is mapped via Euclidean color distance to the official DMC 6-strand cotton floss color catalogue with verified hex codes.

What stitch count fabric is used for physical dimension estimates?

Calculations default to standard 14-count Aida (14 stitches per inch), providing exact finished project dimensions in centimeters and inches.

Can I limit the total number of colors in my pattern?

Yes. The quantization slider lets you restrict the palette between 2 and 64 colors to simplify complex needlework projects.

Privacy & security

Photos are decoded and quantised entirely inside your browser with HTML5 Canvas. Your images, family portraits and craft projects are never uploaded to any server.
