CSV Date Normalizer

Standardize mixed dates into US (MM/DD/YYYY), ISO (YYYY-MM-DD), or European formats. Zero server uploads — all processing happens directly in your browser.

What it does

Scans mixed-format date columns across spreadsheet rows and converts every value into a uniform target convention: ISO (YYYY-MM-DD), US (MM/DD/YYYY), or European (DD/MM/YYYY). Fixes inconsistent formatting resulting from disparate database exports or international data collection.

Common use cases

  • Database Migration Date Normalization: Convert heterogeneous date formats (e.g. 05/12/2023, Dec 5 2023) into ISO 8601 (YYYY-MM-DD).
  • International Date Ambiguity Resolution: Resolve ambiguous dates (like 03/04/2024 as March 4th vs April 3rd) with guided interactive prompts.
  • Spreadsheet Standardization: Harmonize transaction dates across bank statements and accounting exports prior to financial modeling.

How to use

  1. Upload a CSV spreadsheet containing dates written in varying or inconsistent formats.
  2. Select the column index containing date information from your table header.
  3. Choose your target date format standard from the dropdown selection menu.
  4. Click Normalize Dates and download your standardized, clean CSV spreadsheet.

Limits & Considerations

Parses ISO, US, and European formatted date strings. Ambiguous numeric dates (such as 02/03/2024) are parsed adhering to standard convention rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the tool handle ambiguous day/month combinations?

When dates like 02/03/2024 can represent either Feb 3 or March 2, the tool flags the ambiguity and lets you specify the primary regional convention.

What is the default normalized date output format?

Dates standardize to the universal ISO 8601 YYYY-MM-DD standard, which is natively supported by SQL databases, Excel, and analytics tools.

What happens to rows with empty or non-date values?

Blank cells and recognized non-date strings are preserved in place without corrupting adjacent columns or altering row ordering.

Privacy & Security

Data transformation happens strictly inside your local browser instance. No database records or customer logs are uploaded.
