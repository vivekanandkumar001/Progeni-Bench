CSV Date Normalizer

Standardize mixed dates into US (MM/DD/YYYY), ISO (YYYY-MM-DD), or European formats. Zero server uploads — all processing happens directly in your browser.

What it does

Scans mixed-format date columns across spreadsheet rows and converts every value into a uniform target convention: ISO (YYYY-MM-DD), US (MM/DD/YYYY), or European (DD/MM/YYYY). Fixes inconsistent formatting resulting from disparate database exports or international data collection.

How to use

  1. Upload a CSV spreadsheet containing dates written in varying or inconsistent formats.
  2. Select the column index containing date information from your table header.
  3. Choose your target date format standard from the dropdown selection menu.
  4. Click Normalize Dates and download your standardized, clean CSV spreadsheet.

Limits & Considerations

Parses ISO, US, and European formatted date strings. Ambiguous numeric dates (such as 02/03/2024) are parsed adhering to standard convention rules.

Privacy & Security

Data transformation happens strictly inside your local browser instance. No database records or customer logs are uploaded.
