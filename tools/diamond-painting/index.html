Diamond Painting Pattern Maker

Generate symbol-coded diamond painting drill charts — round or square drills, DMC drill counts per colour, a cm canvas planner and a CSV kit shopping list. Zero server uploads — all processing happens directly in your browser.

What it does

Uses the same Pattern Studio pipeline as the cross-stitch tool — average-pooled cells, median-cut reduction and snap-to-DMC — but renders every cell as a drill instead of a stitch. Drills are drawn as filled circles (round) or rounded squares with a highlight (square), each optionally stamped with a bold symbol letter, over a grid highlighted every 10 drills to match the standard drill-counting rulers. A canvas-size planner converts a physical width in centimetres to a drill count at the standard 2.5 mm diamond drill pitch and back, so kits can be planned from a real canvas size. The legend reports the DMC code, colour name, swatch, symbol and per-colour drill total, the grand total drill count and estimated skeins, and the physical canvas dimensions. Export the chart as a PNG (≥2,000 px wide), download a CSV kit shopping list mapping DMC codes to drill counts, or print the chart with its legend.

Common use cases

  • Custom Diamond Painting Canvases: Generate printable adhesive drill canvas charts from personal photos with standard 2.5 mm drill spacing.
  • Mosaic Craft Charting: Plan square or round diamond drill mosaic layouts with high-contrast symbol legends.
  • Drill Kit Inventory Check: Export complete DMC drill code summaries to cross-reference with your existing diamond painting beads.

How to use

  1. Drop your source picture into the upload box — the image never leaves your device.
  2. Pick the drill shape (round or square with highlight), and set the drill width (20–200) and maximum colours (2–64); leave snap-to-DMC on to map drills to real floss codes.
  3. Use the canvas planner to type a canvas width in centimetres and press Plan drills — the engine converts it to a drill count using the ~2.5 mm pitch.
  4. Turn the symbol letter per cell and the grid-every-10-drills guide on or off, and watch the live preview and per-colour drill totals in the legend.
  5. Download the chart PNG, export the CSV shopping list (DMC code → drill count), or print the chart and legend for your work surface.

Limits & Considerations

Charts are capped at a 200×200 drill grid (40,000 drills) with the same automatic reduction for oversized or very tall photos. Drill colour codes follow DMC floss names — proprietary AB drill numbering is not included. The canvas planner assumes the standard 2.5 mm drill pitch; larger or smaller drills change the physical size. This produces a printable chart, not a plotted adhesive canvas or a cutting-machine file.

Frequently Asked Questions

What physical drill dimension does the chart use?

The grid is calibrated precisely for standard 2.5 mm diamond drills, matching universal commercial craft canvas specifications.

How are symbols assigned to different drill colors?

The engine assigns distinct, high-contrast alphanumeric symbols and geometric shapes to each color cluster to ensure legible chart printing.

Are diamond painting color numbers identical to embroidery floss?

Yes. Diamond painting manufacturing standards use the classic DMC color numbering system, allowing direct crossover with standard thread charts.

Privacy & Security

Colour reduction, symbol assignment and drill rendering all execute in local browser memory with HTML5 Canvas. Your photos and kit plans are never sent to a remote server.
