Embroidery Pattern Simplifier

Reduce artwork to a simplified thread palette — median-cut to N colours, optional DMC matching or a pasted custom hex palette, with a side-by-side smooth preview and skein estimates. Zero server uploads — all processing happens directly in your browser.

What it does

A dedicated colour-reduction studio rather than a stitch chart. Your image is average-pooled, clustered with median-cut quantisation to any thread count from 2 to 64, and either left as exact clustered colours or snapped to the shared DMC floss palette. You can also paste your own hex palette (one colour per line) to match non-DMC threads — ideal for planning from a specific thread set or a fabric swatch. The preview shows the original photograph beside a smooth, non-gridded simplified render so you can judge the reduction before committing, and the legend lists every thread's code or hex, name, swatch, share of the design as a percentage and an estimated skein count (~1,700 stitches per skein). Export a smooth simplified reference image (≥2,000 px wide), a stitched grid chart PNG, or a CSV palette report with hex, DMC code/name, count, percentage and skeins.

Common use cases

  • Hand Embroidery Pattern Tracing: Simplify complex illustrations into flat color blocks and crisp boundary lines for transfer to linen or cotton fabric.
  • Applique Patch Planning: Reduce intricate multi-tone graphics into manageable shapes for fabric cutting and satin-stitch edging.
  • Thread Painting Outlines: Isolate primary color zones to plan thread blending and needle direction for embroidery projects.

How to use

  1. Drag and drop the illustration or photo you want to reduce onto the upload box.
  2. Set the thread count with the Max colours slider (2–64) — median-cut clustering does the colour reduction.
  3. Leave Snap to DMC on for real floss codes, or paste your own hex list into the custom palette box and press Apply custom palette to match other threads.
  4. Compare the original and the smooth simplified preview side by side, and read the per-colour area percentages and estimated skeins in the legend.
  5. Export the smooth simplified PNG for reference, a grid chart PNG for stitching, or the CSV palette report for your floss shopping list.

Limits & Considerations

Designed for hand-embroidery colour planning and floss selection; it does not generate machine-embroidery files such as DST, PES or JEF, and it does not auto-trace stitch direction or shading. Custom palettes accept up to 64 hex colours. Reduction is capped at the same 200×200 cell grid as the other pattern tools, and median-cut produces representative colours rather than a perfectly perceptually uniform palette.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the pattern simplifier differ from the cross-stitch tool?

While cross-stitch creates a rigid pixelated grid, the embroidery simplifier reduces color gradients into continuous organic color regions suitable for satin and fill stitches.

Does the tool support dithering to blend color transitions?

Yes. You can toggle error-diffusion dithering on or off depending on whether you prefer smooth color blending or hard, clean stitch boundaries.

How can I transfer the finished design onto fabric?

Export a high-contrast black-and-white line version or color guide, print it at 100% scale, and transfer using water-soluble stabilizer or heat-transfer pens.

Privacy & Security

All clustering, matching and export run locally in your browser via HTML5 Canvas. Your original artwork, textile concepts and photographs stay on your device.
