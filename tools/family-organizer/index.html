Frequently asked questions

What tokens can I use in the custom rename pattern?

You can use tokens for YYYY (year), MM (month), DD (day), original filename, title, and sequential collision numbers.

What happens if two photos share the same date and title?

The engine automatically appends zero-padded collision avoidance numbers (-01, -02) so filenames stay unique.

Are original files modified on my hard drive without confirmation?

No. Browser security prevents direct disk modification; you review the live rename plan and download the renamed files intentionally.

Privacy & security

All photo analysis and document compilation occur inside your local browser sandbox. No family pictures are ever uploaded to external servers.
