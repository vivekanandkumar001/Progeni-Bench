What it does

Runs a Sheet Studio engine tuned for heat-transfer paper: a live canvas preview built from the exact layout the exports use, with a one-click Mirror all images toggle that is ON by default (the core iron-on requirement) and per-image mirror overrides in the thumbnail strip. Three preset profiles configure the sheet in one step — Light transfer paper (mirror), Dark transfer paper (no mirror) and HTV vinyl (mirror + cut lines) — and cut-line guides draw on every cell in the preview with an option to bake them into the export for scissor or plotter trimming. Choose Letter or A4 paper, margin, gap and 1-6 columns; designs never mirror across cells — each flips in place about its own center. Exports a 300 DPI PNG (150 DPI draft option) or prints with exact @page sizing.

Common use cases

  • Iron-on Gang Sheet Creation: Pack multiple heat transfer designs onto a single US Letter or A4 sheet to minimize specialty paper waste.
  • Light & Dark Fabric Presets: Rapidly toggle between mirrored mode for light transfer paper and unmirrored mode with cut outlines for dark transfer media.
  • Team Apparel & Uniform Numbering: Lay out matching front crests, sleeve badges, and jersey numbers across multiple printable pages.

How to use

  1. Pick a preset profile — Light transfer paper (mirror), Dark transfer paper (no mirror), or HTV vinyl (mirror + cut lines) — or toggle Mirror all images yourself; it starts ON because light transfer paper and HTV are applied face-down.
  2. Drop multiple PNG/JPEG designs into the upload box; thumbnails appear in the strip with a ⇄ badge that cycles each image between automatic, forced-mirrored and never-mirrored.
  3. Choose Letter or A4 paper and set margin, gap and columns (1-6) while watching the live sheet preview and page tabs for multi-page jobs.
  4. Enable cut lines to see dashed guides around every design, and optionally bake them into the exported PNG for trimming.
  5. Download a 300 DPI PNG or press Print — always print at 100% / Actual size, then press face-down so the mirrored artwork reads correctly.

Limits & Considerations

Mirrors each design horizontally in place (no rotation, color separation or CMYK halftone screening). Cut lines are rectangular guides around each cell — irregular artwork silhouettes are not auto-traced. Up to 40 images per session with automatic multi-page flow; print at 100% scale, not 'Fit to page'.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the tool mirror artwork by default upon upload?

Light fabric heat transfer paper and HTV require face-down application, which means the design must be inverted prior to printing to read forward on fabric.

Can I override mirroring for individual graphics on the sheet?

Yes. Every image thumbnail features an override badge allowing you to toggle between automatic mirror, forced mirror, or forward-facing display.

What export resolution does the iron-on sheet maker produce?

Exports render at a full 300 DPI for crisp photographic transfers, with an optional 150 DPI draft setting for rapid layout testing.

Privacy & Security

All mirroring, compositing and PNG export happen in local browser memory using HTML5 Canvas. Your artwork, logos and designs are never uploaded to any server.
