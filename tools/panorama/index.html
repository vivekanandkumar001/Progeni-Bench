Panorama Carousel Splitter

Seamlessly slice wide panoramic photos into 3 seamless square Instagram carousel tiles. Zero server uploads — all processing happens directly in your browser.

Quick answer

Panorama Carousel Splitter is a free images tool that runs in your browser. Splits ultra-wide panoramic photographs into 3 mathematically seamless square image tiles.

What it does

Splits ultra-wide panoramic photographs into 3 mathematically seamless square image tiles. Enables photographers and travelers to publish swipeable multi-slide panoramic carousels on Instagram and social feeds without ugly compression or letterboxing.

Common use cases

  • Instagram Carousel Panoramas: Split wide panoramic landscape shots into 2 to 6 seamless swipeable carousel slides.
  • Multi-Post Photo Grids: Divide ultra-wide photography into contiguous square or 4:5 vertical photo posts.
  • Print Triptych Planning: Segment panoramic cityscape or nature photography into multi-panel wall art triptychs.

How to use

  1. Drag and drop an ultra-wide panoramic landscape photo file into the splitter.
  2. The splitter segments the image width into 3 mathematically seamless adjacent square tiles.
  3. Inspect the 3 carousel tiles rendered side-by-side in the preview pane.
  4. Click each tile to download the individual high-resolution square PNG image files.

Limits & Considerations

Slices images into 3 equal square segments. Requires wide landscape images with aspect ratio of at least 3:1 for optimal square proportions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will there be gaps or visible seams between the split tiles?

No. The splitter cuts pixels with edge-to-edge precision, ensuring seamless transitions when swiping on Instagram.

What aspect ratios are supported for carousel slides?

You can divide into standard 1:1 squares or 4:5 vertical portrait tiles, which offer maximum screen real estate on mobile feeds.

How are the split tiles numbered for upload?

Files are exported with sequential numbered filenames (e.g., tile-1.png, tile-2.png) to simplify multi-photo carousel uploads.

Privacy & Security

Image slicing executes locally in Canvas memory. Your original landscape and vacation photos are never uploaded to any cloud server.
