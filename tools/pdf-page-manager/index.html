About this tool — how it works, limits & FAQ

What it does

Builds a new PDF from the pages you choose, in the exact order shown in the editor. PDF.js reads documents and renders on-demand thumbnails; pdf-lib copies source pages, inserts blank pages and images, and writes the finished PDF entirely in browser memory. Original files are never modified or uploaded.

Common use cases

  • Reorder or remove pages from reports, scanned documents and study materials.
  • Combine selected pages from a second PDF or append another document.
  • Add blank sheets or PNG/JPEG pages before printing or sharing a document.

How to use

  1. Choose or drop a PDF. Review its page count and select the pages you want to change.
  2. Drag pages to reorder them, or use the accessible move buttons. Delete or duplicate selected pages, or undo and redo edits.
  3. Insert pages from another PDF, append the whole document, add a blank page, or insert PNG/JPEG images. Choose the insertion point and page size.
  4. Export the edited document, review the output page count, and download the validated PDF.

Limits & considerations

Accepts unencrypted PDFs up to 50 MB and 150 pages per source; output is limited to 250 pages. A second PDF is limited to 30 MB. Image insertion supports PNG and JPEG. Digital signatures are invalidated by any edit; links, annotations, bookmarks, form fields, metadata and other advanced PDF features may not be preserved. Always compare exported files when those features matter.

Frequently asked questions

Are my documents uploaded?

No. Reading, thumbnail rendering, page editing and PDF export happen locally in browser memory. The tool makes no document-processing network requests.

Can I edit an encrypted PDF?

Password-protected PDFs are not supported. The tool reports that restriction without attempting to bypass document protection.

Will links, forms, bookmarks and signatures survive?

Digital signatures are invalidated when a PDF is edited. Links, annotations, bookmarks, forms, metadata and other advanced features may be lost; this tool does not promise to preserve them.

Privacy & security

PDF and image bytes are read and processed only in your browser. The editor does not call a PDF-processing API, send document contents to analytics, or persist files in localStorage or IndexedDB. Temporary thumbnails and output URLs are released when replaced or when you leave the page.
