Photo Print Border Calculator

Add a precise mat border for 4×6, 5×7, 8×10, 8×11 or A4 frames at 200/300 DPI — fixed mm or % borders, custom colour and an optional mat keyline. Zero server uploads — all processing happens directly in your browser.

What it does

Runs an Image Studio border engine that places your photo onto a real physical target — 4×6, 5×7, 8×10, 8×11, A4 or a custom size in mm or inches — and renders the result at a chosen 200 or 300 DPI, sized to the physical target. In Fit-inside mode the photo is scaled to sit inside the target with a uniform border, so a 4:5 photo prints inside a 5×7 without any edge cropping; in Fit-to-exact-target mode the photo crops to fill the canvas edge to edge. The border width is set in fixed millimetres or as a percentage of the short side, the colour is white, black or a custom pick, and an optional mat effect draws a thin keyline around the photo. A live report shows the output pixels, the physical size and the exact border and printed-photo dimensions, and the result exports as PNG or JPEG.

Common use cases

  • Framing & Mat Simulation: Add clean 1/2 inch or 15 mm white mat borders to digital photos before printing for 4x6, 5x7, or 8x10 standard frames.
  • Gallery Exhibition Keylines: Inset a delicate dark keyline between image and mat to enhance subject separation in fine art photography prints.
  • Social Print Cropping: Format artwork for square or rectangular wall mounts without altering the native master image files.

How to use

  1. Drop a photo and pick the target print size: 4×6, 5×7, 8×10, 8×11, A4 or Custom (switch the units between mm and inches).
  2. Choose the fitting mode: Fit inside for a uniform border around the whole photo, or Fit to exact target (crop to fill) to fill the canvas edge to edge.
  3. Set the border width in fixed mm or as a % of the short side, and pick white, black or a custom border colour.
  4. Enable the mat effect to add a thin keyline around the photo, and choose the output DPI (200 or 300).
  5. Read the on-screen report to confirm the physical dimensions, then download a PNG or JPEG — print at 100% / actual size for the stated measurements to hold true.

Limits & Considerations

Creates rectangular borders and mats only — no textured or beveled frames, drop shadows or rounded corners. Border percentages are relative to the shorter target side; print at 100% scale for the reported millimetre sizes to be accurate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate border width in millimeters vs percentage?

Select millimeters for an exact physical ruler measurement, or percentage to automatically scale the border proportional to the shorter side of the target.

Will the tool upscale low-resolution photos?

The tool renders output at your specified 200 or 300 DPI target canvas dimensions, displaying live pixel readouts so you can verify source clarity.

Can I choose custom mat colors beyond black and white?

Yes. An integrated color picker lets you sample any custom hex tint or match specific archival paper stock colors.

Privacy & Security

Borders and mats are composited locally with HTML5 Canvas. Your personal photographs are never uploaded or stored on any server.
