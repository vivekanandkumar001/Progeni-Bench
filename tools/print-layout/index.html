Print Layout Optimizer

Pack up to 40 photos onto Letter, A4, 4×6 or A3 paper with aspect-aware grids, auto-pack rows, mm borders and a printed-size report. Zero server uploads — all processing happens directly in your browser.

What it does

Runs a Sheet Studio layout engine with two packing modes: a uniform grid with selectable rows and columns whose cells fill the sheet minus margins (photos fit inside with their aspect ratio preserved and are letterboxed in the border color), and an auto-pack mode that greedily fills rows by aspect ratio at a computed target row height so mixed portrait and landscape shots waste almost nothing. Photos auto-rotate 90° when that fills the cell better. Margins, gaps and per-photo borders are controlled in millimeters with white, black or a custom border color, and the result is reported on screen (e.g. each photo printed at ≈ 102 × 136 mm). Supports Letter, A4, 4×6 and A3 paper, 150 or 300 DPI PNG export, and direct printing with exact @page sizing.

Common use cases

  • Photo Album Printing: Efficiently tile mixed portrait and landscape snapshots onto A4 or Letter photo paper with zero overlap.
  • Artisan Mini Print Sheets: Create uniform grids of miniature art prints, flashcards, or trading cards with millimeter-exact border spacing.
  • Contact Sheet Documentation: Compile proof sheets of up to 40 images with printed size metrics before making final large-format prints.

How to use

  1. Choose your paper (Letter, A4, 4×6 photo paper or A3) and a pack mode: Uniform grid for equal cells, or Auto-pack rows to let the engine fill rows by each photo's aspect ratio.
  2. Add up to 40 photos from the upload box — a progress note tracks loading and extras beyond 40 are skipped.
  3. Set margin, gap and border width in mm, pick white/black/custom border color, and toggle auto-rotate if landscape shots should turn 90° to fill their cell.
  4. Check the live preview, page tabs and the meta line for the resulting physical photo size before you print.
  5. Download a 150 or 300 DPI PNG, or press Print — always print at 100% / Actual size for true dimensions.

Limits & Considerations

Packs rectangular photos only — no freehand collage, diagonal rotation, polygonal cropping or rounded corners. In grid mode all photos share one cell size (varying aspects letterbox differently), while auto-pack mode sizes rows by aspect; up to 40 photos per session, flowing across multiple pages. Print at 100% scale for the reported mm sizes to hold true.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Uniform Grid and Auto-Pack mode?

Uniform Grid creates identical cell dimensions for all photos, whereas Auto-Pack dynamically packs mixed aspect ratios into tight rows to minimize blank margins.

Does the layout optimizer crop my original photographs?

No. In fit-inside mode, images preserve their exact native aspect ratios and are letterboxed cleanly within your chosen border color.

What paper formats are supported for print export?

Standard presets include US Letter, A4, A3, and 4x6 inch photographic paper with direct browser @page print formatting and 300 DPI PNG export.

Privacy & Security

Your photos are processed entirely in local browser memory with HTML5 Canvas and never leave your device — no server sees your images.
