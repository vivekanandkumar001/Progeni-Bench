Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Uniform Grid and Auto-Pack mode?

Uniform Grid creates identical cell dimensions for all photos, whereas Auto-Pack dynamically packs mixed aspect ratios into tight rows to minimize blank margins.

Does the layout optimizer crop my original photographs?

No. In fit-inside mode, images preserve their exact native aspect ratios and are letterboxed cleanly within your chosen border color.

What paper formats are supported for print export?

Standard presets include US Letter, A4, A3, and 4x6 inch photographic paper with direct browser @page print formatting and 300 DPI PNG export.
