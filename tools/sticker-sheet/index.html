🏷️Sticker Sheet Maker

Build printable sticker sheets in your browser — custom grids, shapes, kiss-cut lines, and PNG / PDF / SVG die-line export for Cricut, Silhouette, or home printers. Zero server uploads — all processing happens directly in your browser.

Quick answer

What it does

Arranges any number of transparent PNG or JPEG graphics onto a precise multi-column sticker layout on US Letter, A4, A3 or 4x6 paper at 300 DPI. Every sticker can be fitted (contain or cover), masked to a square, rounded or true-circle shape, outlined with a white kiss-cut border, extended with print bleed, and traced with dashed or solid cut lines plus optional corner registration marks for Print-Then-Cut workflows. A per-sticker editor gives you rotate, flip, size control (with live mm readout) and automatic transparent-edge trimming, and the whole sheet supports undo/redo, saved projects, zoom with a tilt-able 3D paper preview, multi-copy sheets, browser printing, and PNG / PDF / SVG die-line export.

Common use cases

  • Cricut & Silhouette Print-Then-Cut: Arrange multiple custom sticker graphics on US Letter or A4 with corner registration marks for automated vinyl and paper cutting.
  • Small Business Product Packaging: Format logo decals, thank-you stickers, and branded bag seals on multi-column adhesive paper sheets.
  • Journaling & Planner Accessories: Pack custom hand-drawn characters, icon sets, and decorative washi strips onto 4x6 or A4 sticker sheets.

How to use

  1. Choose your paper standard (US Letter, A4, A3, or 4x6 photo paper) and set columns, rows, spacing, margins, and bleed — or leave columns on Auto to fit everything on one page.
  2. Drag and drop your PNG, JPEG, WebP, or SVG graphics into the upload box with a live loading bar. There is no 12-image limit: extra designs automatically flow onto additional pages, and Sheet copies repeats every sticker up to 4 times.
  3. Style the sheet: contain or cover fit, square / rounded / circle shape, white kiss-cut border width, dashed or solid cut lines, cut line color, background color, and optional corner registration marks.
  4. Click the pencil on any thumbnail to open the per-sticker editor: rotate, flip, resize with mm readout, trim transparent edges, replace, duplicate, or delete. Drag thumbnails to reorder (or Ctrl+Arrow keys), and undo any step with Ctrl+Z.
  5. Preview flat in 2D with zoom/pan or switch to the 3D tilt view, then download a 300 DPI PNG, a print-ready multi-page PDF, an SVG die-line for Cricut / Silhouette, or send the sheet straight to your printer. Save the project in your browser or export it as JSON.

Limits & Considerations

Cut lines follow the chosen sticker shape (square, rounded, circle) — the tool does not auto-trace irregular artwork silhouettes into vector contours. PNG and SVG export the page currently shown (or all pages at once); PDF bundles every page into one file. Saved projects use this browser's local storage; use Export JSON to move a project between devices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sticker Sheet Maker upload my artwork to an external server?

No. All image placement, bleed calculation, masking, and export generation happen entirely inside your web browser memory using HTML5 Canvas.

What export formats are available for digital cutting machines?

You can download 300 DPI print-ready PNG sheets, multi-page vector PDFs, or SVG die-lines containing exact cut path contours for Cricut Design Space and Silhouette Studio.

Can I adjust individual stickers independently on the sheet?

Yes. The built-in per-sticker editor allows you to rotate, mirror, resize with millimeter readouts, trim transparent borders, and reorder items via drag-and-drop.

Privacy & Security

All canvas operations and image composing execute locally in your web browser memory. Your illustrations and design assets are never transmitted to external cloud servers.
