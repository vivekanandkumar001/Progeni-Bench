Subtitle Reading-Speed Checker

Audit SRT subtitles for high Characters-Per-Second (CPS), duration, and overlaps. Zero server uploads — all processing happens directly in your browser.

What it does

Audits SubRip (.srt) subtitle files to identify caption cues that exceed broadcast standards (such as Netflix and BBC guidelines of 21 characters per second). Flags cues with insufficient reading duration, zero-duration errors, and overlapping timecodes to ensure accessibility for viewers.

How to use

  1. Upload your SubRip (.srt) subtitle caption file into the reading speed auditor.
  2. The engine calculates active duration, character counts, and CPS metrics for every subtitle cue.
  3. Inspect highlighted cues flagged for excessive reading speed or timecode collision errors.
  4. Use the audit summary to adjust caption timing and line splits in your subtitle editor.

Limits & Considerations

Audits SubRip SRT subtitle files. Does not directly extract proprietary binary subtitle streams embedded in container formats like MKV or MP4.

Privacy & Security

Subtitle files are parsed purely within client-side memory. Confidential movie scripts, documentary translations, and transcripts remain strictly private.
