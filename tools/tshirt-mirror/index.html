T-Shirt Print Mirror

Flip art horizontally, vertically or 180° for heat-transfer paper — SVG passthrough, before/after preview, batch download and PNG/JPEG/WebP export. Zero server uploads — all processing happens directly in your browser.

What it does

Runs an Image Studio flip engine. Choose a horizontal flip (the classic heat-transfer mirror), a vertical flip, or a 180° rotation, and check the result in a before/after side-by-side preview before you print. Raster art exports as PNG (lossless), JPEG or WebP with a quality slider and an optional white-background flatten. Uploaded SVGs can pass straight through in vector mode: the file is re-serialised with a mirrored so cutting machines and vinyl plotters keep true paths instead of a rasterised PNG. Multiple files can be flipped and downloaded one by one in batch, and a 'flipped sheet' hand-off note lists the mirrored filenames and explains how to feed them into the Iron-on Transfer Sheet Maker.

How to use

  1. Drop one or more graphics — PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG — into the upload box (batch works on the whole set).
  2. Pick the flip axis: Horizontal for transfer paper, Vertical, or 180° rotation.
  3. Check the Before / After panels; toggle Flatten onto white background for JPEG export, or leave SVG passthrough on to keep vectors.
  4. Set the export format (PNG, JPEG or WebP) and quality, then press Download flipped for the active file.
  5. Use Download all for a batch, or grab the hand-off note to continue in the Iron-on Transfer Sheet Maker with mirroring already applied.

Limits & Considerations

Flips raster art at original pixel dimensions and rotates SVGs inside their viewBox; it does not perform colour separations or CMYK halftone dot screening for screen-printing. Batch mode downloads files one at a time (allow multiple downloads in your browser). Up to 30 files per session.

Privacy & Security

Flips, SVG re-serialisation and canvas export all run in local browser memory. Your apparel designs and logos are never uploaded to any server.
