vCard VCF Split and Merge

Merge multiple .vcf contact cards or inspect contact files privately. Zero server uploads — all processing happens directly in your browser.

What it does

Parses RFC 6350 vCard files, handles multi-line folded fields, and enables users to inspect individual contact cards or combine multiple .vcf files into one master archive. Facilitates seamless contact migration across Android, Apple iOS, Microsoft Outlook, and Google Contacts.

How to use

  1. Select one or multiple .vcf contact cards from your device storage.
  2. The parser unfolds continuation lines and displays all detected contact records.
  3. Download individual .vcf cards or export a single combined master .vcf file.
  4. Import the resulting clean contact file into Apple Contacts, Outlook, or Google Contacts.

Limits & Considerations

Supports vCard versions 2.1, 3.0, and 4.0 text structures. Embedded base64 contact photos are preserved but not previewed as visual thumbnails.

Privacy & Security

Your private address book and personal contact cards remain strictly on your machine. Client-side parsing ensures total privacy.
