Video Thumbnail Contact Sheet

Extract 4–48 timestamped frames into a configurable PNG/JPEG contact sheet. Zero server uploads — all processing happens directly in your browser.

What it does

Scans a local video file and extracts between 4 and 48 evenly spaced frames into a contact sheet whose grid columns, per-thumbnail resolution, padding and background colour you control. Every frame is stamped with a burned-in MM:SS.mmm timestamp and frame index so scenes can be located precisely, and each seek is raced against a timeout with an automatic previous-frame fallback, so an unresponsive codec never hangs the sheet. Export the sheet as PNG or JPEG (with quality control) and download a CSV of the sampled timestamps.

Common use cases

  • Video Scene Indexing: Extract evenly spaced thumbnail sequences from video footage to create visual contact sheets and scene indexes.
  • Client Video Review Proofs: Produce single-page summary sheets of video takes, B-roll reels, and commercial cuts for fast sign-off.
  • Video Archive Cataloging: Document video content libraries with timestamped visual summaries for local drive archives.

How to use

  1. Drop an MP4, WebM or MOV video into the extractor, or click Add file and pick one.
  2. Choose the frame count (4–48), grid columns, thumbnail width, padding, background colour and whether to burn timestamp labels.
  3. Watch the progress bar as the browser seeks through the timeline, then inspect the assembled contact sheet on screen.
  4. Download the sheet as PNG or JPEG, or grab the CSV of sampled timestamps for editing notes.

Limits & Considerations

Requires browser-supported codecs (H.264, VP8, VP9, AV1). High-bitrate ProRes, DNxHD, raw AVI, HEVC-in-MKV and DRM-protected files cannot be decoded. Seeks that time out after 8 seconds fall back to the previous frame and are reported as warnings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What video formats can be processed in the browser?

Any video codec supported natively by your browser (including MP4 with H.264, WebM with VP8/VP9, and modern AV1 files) can be indexed.

Can I control how many thumbnail frames are captured?

Yes. You can specify capture intervals or total frame count across the duration of the video.

Are timecodes stamped onto each extracted thumbnail?

Yes. The contact sheet bakes exact SMPTE or HH:MM:SS timecodes into each thumbnail cell for precise video navigation.

Privacy & Security

Video decoding, seeking and frame rendering occur entirely through local browser hardware acceleration. Zero bytes of your video footage are sent to any server.
